Snorlax Meal Planner

Finds the teams and the three meals a day that give Snorlax the most strength in a week, counting berries, favourite berries, island bonus, cooking and Sunday's bigger pot and crit chance. Reliability is the chance that one day of helping covers every ingredient the day's meals need.

Day and helping

Check-ins: the planner assumes you empty each Pokémon's bag this many times plus once at the end of the day, so a higher number means less capping and more swap opportunities. If you check in more often than this, the real result will be at least as good as the plan. Average energy speed: how much energy shortens the time between helps (0.45 is full energy, the fastest, and 1 is empty). The defaults, 0.55 by day and 0.78 overnight, come from simulating a typical team: energy drains while you sleep, so overnight helping is slower. They are only used for the first, broad search; the top builds are then re-run with a proper energy simulation, which shows the figures it found on each card. Energy skills are modelled on top of these baseline figures.

Ranking uses cooking plus berries. Skill strength (Charge Strength, Berry Burst and similar) is estimated and shown as a bonus unless you tick the box above. Energy speed multiplies your help interval: 0.45 at 80+ energy, 0.52 at 60+, 0.58 at 40+, 0.66 below that, so lower is faster. The page uses one average per period instead of simulating your energy. Tune these until the helps per day above match Raenonx for one of your Pokémon.

Sync across devices (optional)

Keeps your box and settings in a file in your own GitHub repository, so every device you open this page on shows the same data. Changes are saved automatically a moment after you make them.

Setup steps
  1. Create a public repository on GitHub and upload this page to it as index.html. Then open Settings, then Pages, and publish from the main branch.
  2. On GitHub, open Settings, Developer settings, Personal access tokens, Fine-grained tokens, and create one. Set Repository access to Only select repositories and choose this repository. Under Repository permissions, set Contents to Read and write.
  3. Open your published page, fill in the fields below, paste the token and press Connect. Do this once on each device.

The token is stored only in this browser and is never included in backups. Without a token the page can still load your data from a public repository, but it cannot save changes.

Backup

Your box, settings, island percentages and recipe levels are saved in this browser, but browsers can clear that. Copy this text somewhere safe and paste it back here to restore everything, including on another device.

Game data and production formulas come from Sleep API (Neroli's Lab), Apache-2.0. Only direct-strength skills and Berry Burst are modelled. Ingredient-producing and other support skills are not.
